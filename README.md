# Canada Laws Website

This is a simple website that presents Canadian laws in an accessible format. It includes a collection of legal documents, resources, and links to important government websites regarding Canadian law.

## Features

- **Canadian Laws Database**: A collection of key legal documents and statutes.
- **Easy Navigation**: Designed for users to quickly find relevant legal information.
- **Responsive Design**: The website is designed to work across a range of devices, including desktop, tablet, and mobile.
  
## How to Use

1. Visit the site at [https://ibeiwasibeen.github.io/canada_laws/](https://ibeiwasibeen.github.io/canada_laws/) to access the laws.
2. Navigate through the different sections of Canadian law available on the site.
3. Links to government websites and legal resources are included for further reading.
